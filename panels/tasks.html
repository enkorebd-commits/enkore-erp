How the score works
Points for a task are weight x timeliness x quality. Weight is Must 3, Should 2, Later 1. Timeliness is 1.0 on time, 0.7 up to two days late, 0.4 beyond that. Quality is 1.0 clean, 0.6 after one return, 0.3 after two.

Task score is points earned divided by points assigned. KPI is 0.5 task score + 0.3 checkpoints + 0.2 (100 − slip rate). Only work the task giver has accepted earns points.
